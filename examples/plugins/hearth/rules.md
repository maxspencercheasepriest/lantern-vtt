# Rolling the dice
When the outcome is uncertain, pick the attribute that fits what you're doing and roll a d6 for each point in it. Keep the highest die.

- **6:** it works.
- **4 or 5:** it works, but there's a cost: harm, lost time, a worse position, something used up.
- **1 to 3:** trouble. It doesn't work, or it works and something bad happens too.
- **Two or more 6s:** better than hoped.

With 0 in the attribute, roll 2 dice and keep the **lowest**.

Help from a friend adds a die; they share the cost.

# Grit and dread
**Grit** is how much more you can take. Lose it from harm and hard choices; get it back by resting somewhere safe.

At 0 grit you're out of the scene: hurt, fled or captured, the group decides.

**Dread** fills as the dark presses in. When the last box fills, you must leave, break down or do something desperate. A night by a hearth clears two boxes.

# Conditions
- **Wounded:** one fewer die on anything physical.
- **Shaken:** one fewer die on anything that needs a cool head.
- **Spent:** you can't help others until you rest.

# Gear
Gear with **uses** runs out: tick a box each time it gets you out of trouble. A full track means it's gone or broken.

# Luck rolls
When nobody is acting but the GM wants to know how the world falls, roll one die.

- **5 or 6:** fortune smiles.
- **3 or 4:** mixed luck.
- **1 or 2:** ill luck.

# Progress clocks
Open **Progress clocks** from the Library's Props to track dangers and long tasks. Fill a slice when something moves it forward; when it's full, it happens.
